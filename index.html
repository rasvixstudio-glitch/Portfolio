01 — About

design, for me, starts with paying close attention.

i'm a UI/UX and brand designer working independently out of Bangalore since 2023 — before that, learning the ropes across four internships and student design teams in Shivamogga. my background is in computer science, which taught me to think in systems; design is where i learned to make those systems feel human.

i work end to end — discovery, user flows, wireframes, prototypes, UI — and just as often i'm asked to build the brand a product lives inside: the name, the mark, the voice, the guidelines that keep it all consistent months after i've handed it off. more recently, i've folded AI-assisted workflows into that process — for ideation and speed, never in place of judgment.

03 — Design Thinking

every project follows a rhythm, not a rigid formula.

my process adapts to each project's needs, but the core philosophy stays constant — listen deeply, define clearly, explore widely, then refine ruthlessly.

01

Discover

Research, interviews and competitive audits. Understanding the real problem before assuming solutions.

02

Define

Synthesising insights into clear problem statements, personas, and design principles that guide every decision.

03

Ideate

Broad exploration — sketches, moodboards, AI-assisted concept generation. Quantity before quality, then sharp curation.

04

Prototype

Interactive prototypes at the right fidelity — low-fi for flow validation, high-fi for visual sign-off and user testing.

05

Test

Usability testing with real users. Watching what breaks, what confuses, and what delights — then acting on it.

06

Iterate

Refinement cycles driven by data and feedback. The best design is rarely the first one — it's the most evolved.

05 — The Signature Project

the project i want to be known for.

imagined, not shipped yet — but it's the clearest picture of where I want my work to sit.

Concept — Brand Identity + Product Design

Root — a journal for people who garden their attention, not their plants.

root

Root is a small, slow app for tracking the quiet, non-glamorous habits that actually shape a life — reading a chapter, watering an actual plant, sitting with a feeling instead of scrolling past it. Every entry is logged as growth on a personal, ever-changing botanical illustration rather than a streak counter.

I'd design it top to bottom: the name and mark, a restrained editorial visual identity, the onboarding and daily-use flows, and a component system calm enough to open at 11pm without waking you up further. AI-assisted exploration would speed up early concept variations; every interaction and every line of copy would still be decided by hand.

Why This One

  • it's brand and product, inseparably. the identity only works if the interface behaves like it — which is the kind of project I keep gravitating toward.
  • it rewards restraint. no streaks, no guilt, no red badges — the harder, more interesting design problem is motivating someone gently.
  • it's honest about tools. AI speeds up the exploring; the judgment about what stays is still entirely mine.
